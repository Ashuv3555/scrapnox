Certificates

Documents You'll Receive

Two certificates, issued digitally on the VAHAN system, that make your scrapping 100% legal.

Issued on VAHAN

Certificate of Deposit (CoD)

Issued the day your vehicle is accepted at the facility.

  • Unlocks tax concessions & manufacturer discounts
  • Officially ends your ownership liability
  • Tradable document for incentives
Issued in Form 4

Certificate of Vehicle Scrapping (CVS)

Issued after depollution, dismantling and the chassis cut are complete.

  • Official proof of permanent removal
  • Required for RTO deregistration
  • Protects you from future misuse
