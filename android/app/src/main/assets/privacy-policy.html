Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

ScrapNox ("we", "our") values your privacy. This policy explains, in plain language, what information we collect through our website, mobile app and enquiry forms, and how we use it.

What we collect

  • What you give us: name, phone/WhatsApp number, vehicle registration number, pincode and vehicle type — only when you fill our valuation form or contact us.
  • Technical: basic usage data such as page visits and form events (aggregate data used to improve our service). We do not collect device identifiers for tracking.

Why and how it is used

One purpose: to understand your requirement so our team can contact you with a scrap valuation by call, SMS or WhatsApp. We do not build marketing lists, and we never sell or share your number with third parties.

Where the data stays

  • Enquiry details are kept on our own records so our team can follow up with you.
  • On request, you can view, correct or delete your data — write to info@scrapnox.in.

Third parties

  • Hosting/server providers — to store and serve the website and forms.
  • WhatsApp/Meta — when you press our WhatsApp button, the conversation is between you and WhatsApp.
  • Google Fonts — to serve fonts on this website.

Security

We limit data access to team members who need it and take regular backups. We never ask for sensitive details (card numbers, UPI PINs, passwords) in our forms.

Children

Our services are for vehicle owners. We do not knowingly collect enquiries from anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

Changes will be published on this page and the "Last updated" date will move.
